FAQ

I am a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. I match the load to your level and explain technique in videos and on calls. Many clients started from zero.

Do I need equipment or a gym?

Not necessarily. You can train at home, in the gym or outdoors. Tell me what you have and I will build the program around it.

What is the difference between a program and a video-call workout?

A program is a plan you follow on your own using technique videos. A video-call workout means I am on the call and correct you during the session.

Do I have to go on a strict diet?

No. Nutrition is built around your routine and tastes, without exhausting restrictions. A monthly meal plan can be ordered separately.

When will I see results?

It is individual. Clients usually notice the first changes after a few weeks of regular training, and a clear result comes from consistent work over months.
